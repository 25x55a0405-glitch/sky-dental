Emergency Dental Care

Experiencing acute dental pain or trauma? We provide immediate comfort triage and same-day relief appointments to resolve your crisis.

Emergency Priority

Immediate SAME-DAY Pain Relief

Dental emergencies can be highly distressing and demand prompt attention. Whether you suffer from a severe abscess infection, a knocked-out permanent tooth, or broken orthodontics, our triage team is equipped to seat you immediately to stop the pain.

  • Same-day priority bookings for acute toothache relief
  • Immediate microscopic root canals sealing active infections
  • Trauma stabilization restoring fractured dental enamel structures

Emergency Triage Assessment

1. Are you experiencing severe, throbbing pain or facial swelling?
2. Did you suffer physical impact trauma (knocked-out or loose tooth)?
3. Is there continuous bleeding that won't stop after applying pressure?

Emergency Guidelines

What to Do Before Arriving

If you face an immediate crisis, taking fast action at home can significantly increase the chances of saving your tooth structures.

1

Knocked-Out Tooth

Pick up the tooth by the chewing surface (not the root). Rinse gently in water, and place it in a cup of cold milk to preserve cell vitality.

2

Severe Swelling

Apply cold ice compresses to the external jaw tissue to curb facial inflammation. Do NOT place hot items on gums directly.
