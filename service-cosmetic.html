Aesthetic Engineering

Hand-Crafted Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers are microscopic layers of medical-grade ceramic that are chemically fused to the front of your teeth. Meticulously contoured to complement your unique facial anatomy, skin tone, and alignment profile.

  • Ultra-thin 0.3mm prep for maximum preservation of natural enamel
  • Biocompatible IPS e.max glass-ceramics reflecting natural light
  • Stain-proof surfaces impervious to coffee, tea, or red wine

The Process

Your Veneer Journey in 3 Steps

1. 3D Digital Smile Mockup

Using high-resolution intraoral scanners, we create a 3D digital simulation of your target veneers, allowing you to preview your smile before we touch a single tooth.

2. Micro-enamel Sculpting

We perform an ultra-conservative smoothing of natural enamel (less than 0.3mm), capture dynamic impressions, and place medical temporary veneers.

3. Molecular Fusion bonding

Bonding

Your bespoke porcelain veneers are milled in our lab, calibrated under polarized filters, and molecularly bonded to reveal your gorgeous new smile.
