Dental Implants & Restorations

Replace missing roots permanently with surgical-grade titanium implants and high-aesthetic CAD/CAM milled ceramic crowns.

Biological Integration

Osseointegrated Root Replacement

A dental implant is a biocompatible titanium cylinder that fuses directly into your jawbone via a biological phenomenon called osseointegration. This creates an permanent, anchor-solid anchor that stops bone recession and completely restores chewing efficiency.

  • 100% Biocompatible medical titanium preventing foreign body rejection
  • Restores natural jaw bite force without grinding down adjacent teeth
  • Solid structural foundation preventing facial profile collapse

Clinical Stages

Your Permanent Implant Pathway

1. 3D Bone mapping

We capture digital CBCT 3D X-rays to assess precise bone depth and map ideal surgical pathways with absolute microscopic precision.

2. Surgical placement

A micro-incision is performed under precise computer guides to seat the implant post. Comfort-flow numbing ensures zero pain.

3. Ceramic Restoration

Following 3-4 months of bone integration, we attach the titanium abutment and cement your beautiful permanent zirconia crown.
