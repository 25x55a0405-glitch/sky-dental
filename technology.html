Advanced Clinical Technology

Pioneering biological dental solutions in Kurnool through integrated CAD/CAM milling centers, 3D bone diagnostics, and class-B sterilization corridors.

Replaces unpleasant physical molds with 3D optical impressions. Captures oral structures in high definition in under 3 minutes with zero radiation.

Digital Workflow

The Digital Laboratory Timeline

See how we engineer your crown or veneer from the scan to biological seating in under 24 hours.

Step 1: Scan

Intraoral 3D Scan

A digital mapping wand generates highly precise 3D surface files of the treatment area in minutes.

Step 2: Design

CAD Smile Engineering

Our master technicians shape your custom ceramic restoration on CAD software to align with your smile lines.

Step 3: Mill

CAM Lab Milling

The restoration is milled out of solid IPS e.max lithium disilicate glass ceramic blocks with 10-micron precision.

Step 4: Seat

Molecular Bonding

The final crown is stained, glazed in laboratory ovens, and molecularly bonded to reveal your perfect smile.
