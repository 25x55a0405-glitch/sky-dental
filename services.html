Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding our biological treatments, schedules, and insurance policies.

What is "biological dentistry" exactly?

Biological dentistry recognizes that your oral health has a massive impact on your entire bodily immune system. We utilize bio-compatible materials (completely metal-free ceramic & zirconia fillings and implants) that prevent chronic low-grade toxic reactions, preserving both healthy tooth bone structures and systemic wellness.

Is Invisalign treatment painful?

Invisalign aligners are custom 3D printed from flexible SmartTrack material. They do not contain any sharp metal brackets or wires that cut your gums. While you might feel minor pressure during the first 24 hours of starting a new alignment tray, it is substantially more comfortable and less painful than traditional metal braces.

Do you accept healthcare or dental insurance?

Yes, Sky Dental Clinic is fully affiliated with major Indian health insurers and national corporate corporate networks. Our billing team will handle all insurance pre-authorizations and paper filing on your behalf, maximizing your reimbursement coverage.

How long does a titanium dental implant restoration take?

The full implant restoration timeline typically spans 3 to 6 months. The physical implant placement takes under an hour. After placement, we wait 3-4 months to allow the titanium root to fuse completely with your jawbone (osseointegration) before mounting your custom porcelain aesthetic crown.
